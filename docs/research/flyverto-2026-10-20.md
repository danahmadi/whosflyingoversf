# FlyVerto listing research — October 20, 2026

## Placement decision

Updated October 22, 2026: list FlyVerto first in the regular operators and brokers list, using the same presentation as the other providers. Do not give it a featured label or a separate promotional card. Its stated SF focus and coordination service fit visitors who need help turning a banner idea into a brief and quote. List order is not a ranking based on delivery quality or price.

## Primary sources checked live

- https://flyverto.com/ — advertises Bay Area creative guidance, operator sourcing, campaign coordination and delivery reporting. The planner accepts requests; it does not reserve flights. Its sample campaign is explicitly illustrative.
- https://flyverto.com/pricing — identifies FlyVerto as a broker. Quotes itemize production, operator flight costs, time over target, optional photography and coordination. Published third-party prices are explicitly not FlyVerto quotes or SF rates.
- https://flyverto.com/how-quotes-work — written quotes follow an independent operator’s written price. Confirmation requires customer approval and operator acceptance. Operators control aircraft, pilots and flight decisions. Weather and cancellation terms are specified in each quote.
- https://flyverto.com/report — explicitly labels its delivery report as a fictional sample, not a completed campaign.

## Evidence limits

Exact-name and domain web searches did not surface relevant independent reviews. The pages establish the advertised service, not delivery history, operator quality, lowest pricing or guaranteed availability. No inquiry was submitted. The public listing identifies the broker role and says the service has not been booked or evaluated.
