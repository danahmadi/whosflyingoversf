Methodology & assumptions

  • Flight hours per day: 4–6 h. Monaco CEO Sam Blond: "~$6,000 / day. This includes 6 hours of flight time, up to 2 of which are from the airport to SF (they have to refuel once during the day)." Low = ~4 h over SF only; high = the full 6 h including transit. LinkedIn, Jun 3 2026
  • Spend per day: $4,500–$9,000 (mid $6,000 = Blond's figure). Low ≈ 6 h at published volume rates (~$750–935/h); high ≈ 6 h at $1,500/h, the rate Nathan Flurry (Rivet) quoted for SF (X, Jul 2 2026). Prices vary by operator, banner type (letter vs. printed billboard) and how many days are booked.
  • Fuel burn: 7–12 gal/h of 100LL avgas. An NTSB report on a PA-18A-150 banner-tow flight says "During banner tow operations the fuel burn off rate is about 8 gallons per hour" (NTSB LAX08CA224). The PA-18-150 manual lists 9 gph at 75% power (manual excerpt). The Champion 7KCAB (e.g. N7589F, registered to Aerial Advertising Services, Livermore) is listed at 6–9 gph at 75% power. A Cessna 180 burns ~13 gph at 75% (AOPA). Towing a banner at high power and low speed raises burn, so 12 gph is the upper bound.
  • CO₂: 8.31 kg per gallon of aviation gasoline, EPA GHG Emission Factors Hub (40 CFR 98 Table C-1), combustion only, not lifecycle (EPA 2025). 100LL also emits lead, which isn't counted here.
  • ADS-B column: real flights detected in the adsb.lol open archive for the tracked tails (low, slow loitering inside an SF box), with brands matched from same-day sightings. "Ambiguous" days are credited to the first brand. Real hours replace the 4–6 h guess wherever a flight was found; ADS-B coverage of small planes is incomplete.
  • Pylon's drone airship is shown in the table but left out of fuel, cost and CO₂.
  • Low estimate = days × 4 h × 7 gph; high estimate = days × 6 h × 12 gph. One plane per advertiser per day.

Other public banner pricing

  • Monaco: ~$6,000/day for 6 h, vs. ~$120k/month for a premium 101 billboard (Blond, above).
  • Nathan Flurry (Rivet): "a banner plane for $1.5k/hr" vs. a $240k/yr billboard on 101 (X).
  • Aerial Messages (national operator): $1,275/h for 2 h, falling to $935/h at 100 h (rates).
  • US Air Ads: ~$625–925/h for campaigns (rates). Fly Sky Ads (NY beaches): $375–700+/h (pricing).
  • FlySigns (Hayward base): aerial billboards printed at $1.15–1.89 per sq ft (FlySigns SF).
  • An unsourced X post claimed "+$10k for a 15 min flight" (X); it conflicts with every figure above.
