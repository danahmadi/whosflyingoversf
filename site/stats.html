Stats and method

A photo can identify a banner. A flight track can show where a plane went. Connecting the two takes more evidence.

Days with a dated sighting

We count each brand once per day, even when several people post about it. We use the date of the sighting when a source specifies one, rather than the date of a later post. These counts describe this archive; they do not measure how often a company flies.

How we handle a report

  1. Keep the source link and the wording the person reported. A quoted slogan is not a transcript we independently verified.
  2. Separate the flight date from the post date. “Yesterday” can establish a date; “last week” cannot establish a single day.
  3. Check the location. A banner at SaaStr in San Mateo does not establish a sighting over San Francisco.
  4. Keep unconfirmed dates visible as undated reports. Corrections can change the counts.

You can inspect the source CSV, contribution notes, or flag an error.

What the flight map shows

The map contains 87 candidate tracks from the adsb.lol archive. The detector looks for tracked aircraft spending at least 45 minutes inside an SF bounding box, with median speed below 85 knots and median altitude below 3,500 feet.

That pattern suggests banner towing. It does not prove that a plane carried a banner. A brand seen on the same date is a lead, so we list it as a possible match without assigning the flight to that advertiser.

The archive covers selected aircraft and dates. Missing tracks do not establish that a flight never happened. Older duration values came from a detector that could include gaps and ground time; we have removed those durations from the public estimates until the raw traces can be reprocessed.

What a day might cost

Monaco CEO Sam Blond reported about $6,000 for six flight hours, including up to two hours of transit. That is one campaign's price; the estimates below use a range around it rather than treating it as every company's rate. Read his account.

Aerial Messages currently lists $1,275 per hour for a two-hour booking and $935 per hour at 100 hours. Ask an operator about transit, banner production and weather cancellations before comparing quotes. Published rates · Booking guide.
