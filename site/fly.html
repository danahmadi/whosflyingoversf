Book a banner

Ask about time over the city, transit and the cost of making the banner. Those details can change the quote.

Start with a short brief

Tell the operator the date, the area you want to reach and the exact words or artwork. Ask for a quote that separates these items:

  • Time above SF and time in transit. Monaco's CEO described a six-hour booking with up to two hours between the airport and the city.
  • The banner itself. Individual letters and a printed billboard have different production costs. Ask about character limits, reuse and lead time.
  • Weather and rescheduling. Ask what happens if fog, wind or airspace restrictions prevent the flight.
  • Proof of the flight. Ask whether you will receive a flight log or photos, and who handles any changes on the day.

Keep the message short enough to read from the ground. Give the operator a phone-sized mockup as well as the full artwork.

Two published price references

Monaco's Sam Blond reported about $6,000 for six flight hours. Aerial Messages lists $2,550 for two hours and lower hourly rates for larger bookings. Neither is a quote for your flight.
